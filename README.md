Project Title
PawMart – Pet Care, Appointment & Online Pet Shop Management System
Technology Stack
Frontend: HTML, CSS
Backend: PHP Laravel
Database: MySQL
Payment Gateway: Stripe
Project Overview
PawMart is a web-based pet care management system designed to help pet owners manage veterinary appointments, grooming appointments, pet profiles, online product purchases, and digital payments from one platform. The system also includes an online pet shop where users can browse products, add items to cart, pay through Stripe, and track their order and payment history.
Features
1. User Dashboard
After login, users will see a dashboard with quick access to pet profiles, vet appointments, grooming appointments, Paw Mart shop, cart, orders, and payment history.
2. Profile & Care Management
Users can manage both their personal profile and their pets' profiles in one place.
3. Video Consultation
The system enables users to communicate with veterinarians through secure real-time video sessions.
4. Vet Appointment Booking
Users can book vet appointments by selecting a pet, service type, appointment date, and preferred time.
5. Vet Service Selection
Users can select vet services such as general checkup, vaccination, surgery consultation, dental care, or emergency care.
6. Grooming Appointment Booking
Users can book grooming appointments for their pets by selecting a date, time, and grooming service.
7. Grooming Service Selection
Users can choose grooming services such as bathing, nail trimming, hair trimming, brushing, and teeth brushing.
8. AI Chatbot with RAG 
The system provides an AI-powered chatbot that assists users with pet care using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). 
9. Appointment Management
Users can manage their veterinary appointments by tracking their status, requesting changes, or cancelling appointments when permitted.
10. AI Health Report Summary
The AI summarizes pet medical records into easy-to-understand reports.
11. Wishlist Management
Users can save products for future purchases.
12. Paw Mart Product Catalog
Users can browse pet products such as food, medicine, grooming items, toys, collars, and accessories.
13. Product Catalog Management
Users can browse, search, and view detailed information about products available in the pet care system.
14. Product Reviews & Ratings
Users can share feedback after purchasing products.
15. Shopping Cart Management
Users can add products to cart, update product quantity, remove products, and view the total amount before checkout.
16. Stripe Online Payment Gateway
Users can complete online payment using Stripe. After successful payment, the order will be confirmed automatically.
17. Order Management
Users can view order information including ordered products, quantity, total amount, order status, and order date.
18. Payment History
Users can view their previous payment records including transaction amount, payment method, payment status, and payment date.
19. Inventory Stock Management
Product stock will automatically decrease after a successful order. Admin can also update stock quantity when new products are added.
20. Low Stock Alert
The system will show a low stock warning when a product quantity goes below the minimum stock level, helping the admin restock products on time.
